1. Floor Tile & Protection Circuit

  • 16x PZT-5A Discs (Parallel): Twist all center ceramic leads to AC1 (Row 10) and all brass rim leads to AC2 (Row 15).
  • 4x M8 Stop Bolts: Calibrate to 2.5 mm gap so loads over 35 kg (up to 300+ kg) bypass the piezo discs.
  • Schottky Bridge (4x 1N5819): Converts AC spikes into DC on the VH (+) rail and Common GND (-) rail.
  • Safety: Place 5.1V Zener (1N4733A) (stripe to VH) + 470 µF/25V tank cap + D5 (1N5819) check diode + 500 mA PTC polyfuse before the 6,600 µF / 0.47F reservoir.

2. "I ❤️ QCU!" 18-LED Sign Wiring

  • 18 Recycled Micro-LEDs: 3 LEDs for "I", 6 Red LEDs for "❤️", 9 Green/White LEDs for "QCU".
  • SW1 (Charge / Fire): Keep OFF while walking to accumulate energy; switch ON to illuminate sign.
  • SW2 (Full / Dim): Selects 1.5 kΩ resistor branch (Full ~25 mA / 125 mW) or 10 kΩ branch (Dim ~3.5 mA / 17.5 mW).
  • Ground Return: All LED cathodes (-) tie back to the breadboard Common GND rail.

3. ESP32-WROOM-32 Safe Sensing & PC Link

  • Common Ground: Wire Supercap (-), 2 Dummy Piezos (-), and breadboard GND directly to ESP32 GND.
  • D34 (Supercap Voltage): Supercap (+) ➔ 100 kΩ ➔ D34 ➔ 100 kΩ ➔ GND (2.0x divider).
  • D32 (Step Counter): 2 Dummy Piezos (+) joined ➔ D32, with 1 MΩ bleed + 3.3V Zener (stripe on top) to GND.
  • USB-C Cable: Powers ESP32 from laptop (0% drain on tile) and streams DATA,step,adc_raw,v_cap to ecostep_app.
